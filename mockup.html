HDB Launches 6,000 New BTO Flats in May 2026 — Here's What You Need to Know

The Housing Development Board has launched its May 2026 Build-To-Order exercise, offering 6,000 flats across four estates. Tengah gets the largest allocation with 2,400 eco-friendly units, while Bayshore offers a limited batch of waterfront flats starting from $480,000. Kallang/Whampoa and Queenstown round out the exercise with mature estate options. Balloting opens 19 May and closes 26 May 2026.
