HDB — Housing & Development Board

HDB Launches 6,000 New BTO Flats in May 2026 — Here's What You Need to Know

The Housing Development Board has launched its May 2026 Build-To-Order exercise, offering 6,000 flats across four estates. Tengah gets the largest allocation with 2,400 eco-friendly units, while Bayshore offers a limited batch of waterfront flats starting from $480,000. Kallang/Whampoa and Queenstown round out the exercise with mature estate options. Balloting opens 19 May and closes 26 May 2026.

About pressrelease.sg

Government news you can trust,
in language you can understand

Singapore's government publishes hundreds of press releases every month. Most are dense, bureaucratic, and hard to read. pressrelease.sg rewrites every one in plain English — without changing a single fact — and always links back to the original source.
🏛️

Official sources, nothing else

Every article on pressrelease.sg originates from an official Singapore government press release published on a .gov.sg domain. We do not aggregate social media, rumours, or third-party reports. If we can't link to the original, we don't publish.
✍️

AI rewrites for clarity, not opinion

We use Claude AI to rewrite bureaucratic language into clear, plain English. The AI is instructed to preserve all facts, figures, dates, and policy details exactly as stated. It does not add analysis, opinion, or interpretation.
🔍

Every article links to its source

A direct link to the original .gov.sg press release appears on every article. Readers can always verify what we've written against what the agency actually said. We consider this non-negotiable.
🏷️

Sponsored content is always labeled

Paid content — sponsored posts and brand press releases — is visually distinct from government-sourced articles and permanently labeled. Editorial and commercial content will never be mixed or made to look identical.
